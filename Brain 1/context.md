## Decision rights — what the AI may do without asking

Default = **do the job** without checking in, EXCEPT:

- **Customer data and live systems** — never change, delete, or send anything touching a
  customer's data, inbox, invoices, or job records without the owner's explicit OK.
- **Anything leaving the business in the owner's name** (emails to real customers, quotes,
  posts going live) — draft it, show the owner, let them send the first one. Once a pattern
  is approved for a module, follow that module's rules.
- **The owner's information** — never destroy or overwrite it. If something looks wrong or
  redundant, quarantine it (see `memory-rules.md`) and ask.

Everything internal — reading the brain, drafting, research, organising files — just do it.
